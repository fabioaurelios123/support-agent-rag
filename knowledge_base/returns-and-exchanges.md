# Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love your gear. If something is not right, you can return or exchange it.

## Return window

Unworn, unwashed items with original tags can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Members of the Trailhead Rewards program get an extended 60-day return window. Footwear must be returned in its original box and must not show outdoor wear on the soles.

## How to start a return

Start a return from the Orders page of your account or ask our support assistant to open a return ticket with your order number and email address. You will receive a prepaid return label by email. Returns within the United States are free; international customers pay a flat $9.95 return shipping fee, deducted from the refund.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse receives your return. Your bank may take an additional 3 to 7 business days to post the credit. Original shipping charges are refunded only if the item was defective or we sent the wrong item.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or color of the same product are free, including shipping both ways. We ship the replacement as soon as the carrier scans your return, so you do not have to wait for the warehouse to receive it. If the new size is out of stock, we refund the item instead.

## Final sale and non-returnable items

Items marked Final Sale, gift cards, personalized or monogrammed products, and opened consumables such as fuel canisters and wax cannot be returned. Underwear and swimwear can be returned only if the hygiene liner is intact.
