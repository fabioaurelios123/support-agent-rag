# Trailhead Rewards & Gift Cards

## Trailhead Rewards membership

Trailhead Rewards is free to join. Members earn 1 point for every dollar spent, and every 200 points converts automatically into a $10 reward certificate. Members also get free standard shipping on every order, early access to sales, and an extended 60-day return window.

## Reward certificates

Reward certificates are emailed and expire 90 days after they are issued. Certificates can be combined with each other but not with promo codes. Points are deducted if an order is returned.

## Gift cards

Digital gift cards are available from $25 to $500 and are delivered by email, either immediately or on a scheduled date. Northwind gift cards never expire and have no fees. Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash except where required by law.

## Lost gift cards

If you lost a gift card email, contact support with the purchaser's email address and the approximate purchase date, and we will resend it. Physical gift cards that are lost or stolen cannot be replaced unless you registered them in your account.
