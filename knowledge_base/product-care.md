# Product Care

Taking care of your gear makes it last longer and keeps the warranty valid.

## Waterproof shells and rain jackets

Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle with a technical wash, close all zippers and do not use fabric softener. Tumble dry on low for 20 minutes to reactivate the water-repellent finish. When water stops beading on the surface, re-treat the jacket with a spray-on DWR product.

## Down jackets and sleeping bags

Wash down products in a front-loading machine with a down-specific soap. Dry on low heat with two or three clean tennis balls to restore loft; drying can take two to three hours. Store sleeping bags uncompressed in the large storage sack provided.

## Wool and merino

Merino base layers can be machine washed cold on a wool cycle and should be laid flat to dry. Avoid bleach and high heat, which shrink and weaken the fibers.

## Leather boots

Remove mud with a soft brush after every hike and let boots dry at room temperature, away from heaters or fires. Condition full-grain leather every few months with a leather conditioner to prevent cracking.
