# Payments & Pricing

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Northwind gift cards. Buy-now-pay-later is available through Klarna in four interest-free installments for orders between $50 and $1,000.

## When you are charged

Your card is authorized when you place the order and charged when the order ships. If an order ships in multiple packages, you are charged for each package as it ships.

## Price adjustments

If an item you bought drops in price within 14 days of delivery, we refund the difference once per item. Contact support with your order number. Price adjustments do not apply to clearance events or to prices offered by other retailers.

## Promo codes

Only one promo code can be applied per order, and codes cannot be combined with Trailhead Rewards certificates. Promo codes cannot be applied to gift cards or to orders that were already placed.

## Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated based on the shipping address and shown before you confirm the order. Tax-exempt organizations can request an exemption by contacting support with their certificate.
