# Contact Us & Store Hours

Northwind Outfitters is an online store with one flagship retail store in Portland, Oregon.

## Customer support hours

Our support team is available by chat and email Monday to Friday from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM Pacific Time, and Saturday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM Pacific Time. The support assistant answers questions and opens tickets 24/7. Email tickets receive a reply within 1 business day.

## Flagship store

The Portland flagship store is at 1200 NW Glisan Street (fictional address) and is open Monday to Saturday from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM and Sunday from 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Online orders can be picked up in store free of charge, usually within 2 hours of ordering.

## Products we carry

We sell outdoor apparel, footwear, packs and camping accessories. We do not sell bicycles, kayaks, firearms or fishing gear.
