# Changing or Cancelling an Order

## Cancellation window

You can cancel an order free of charge within 1 hour of placing it, as long as it has not entered processing. After that, the warehouse may already be picking the order, and the fastest option is to refuse the delivery or return it once it arrives.

## Changing the shipping address

The shipping address can be changed while the order status is "processing". Once the order status is "shipped", we cannot redirect the package, but you can request a delivery change directly with the carrier using the tracking number.

## Changing items, sizes or colors

We cannot add items to an order that has already been placed. To change a size or color before shipment, ask support to cancel the line item and place a new order. After shipment, use a free exchange instead.

## Order statuses explained

Processing means we received your order and are preparing it. Shipped means the package left our warehouse and has a tracking number. Delivered means the carrier confirmed delivery. Cancelled means the order will not ship and any authorization has been released. Backordered means at least one item is temporarily out of stock; we email you an expected ship date and you can cancel a backordered item at any time.
