# Sizing Guide

Northwind apparel uses a regular fit unless the product page says otherwise. If you are between sizes, choose the larger size for layering pieces such as shells and insulated jackets.

## Men's tops and jackets

Chest measurements: S fits 91 to 96 cm (36 to 38 in), M fits 97 to 102 cm (38 to 40 in), L fits 103 to 109 cm (40.5 to 43 in), XL fits 110 to 117 cm (43.5 to 46 in), XXL fits 118 to 125 cm (46.5 to 49 in).

## Women's tops and jackets

Bust measurements: XS fits 79 to 83 cm (31 to 32.5 in), S fits 84 to 88 cm (33 to 34.5 in), M fits 89 to 94 cm (35 to 37 in), L fits 95 to 101 cm (37.5 to 39.5 in), XL fits 102 to 109 cm (40 to 43 in).

## Pants and inseams

Pants are sized by waist in inches and come in three inseam lengths: short (30 in), regular (32 in) and long (34 in). Hiking pants have a gusseted crotch and a slightly relaxed thigh.

## Footwear

Our hiking boots run true to size in US sizing. If you wear thick wool socks or have wide feet, go up half a size. Trail runners run half a size small. Wide (2E) widths are available for the Ridgeline boot.

## How to measure

Measure your chest at the fullest point with arms relaxed, your waist at the natural waistline and your inseam from the crotch to the floor without shoes. Keep the tape snug but not tight.
