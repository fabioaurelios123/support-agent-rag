# Shipping & Delivery

Northwind Outfitters ships from our fulfillment center in Portland, Oregon. Orders placed before 1:00 PM Pacific Time on a business day leave the warehouse the same day.

## Domestic shipping (United States)

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over $75. Below $75, standard shipping costs a flat $6.95. Expedited shipping takes 2 business days and costs $14.95. Overnight shipping is available for $29.95 on orders placed before 11:00 AM Pacific Time. We do not deliver on Sundays or US federal holidays.

## International shipping

We ship to Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia and New Zealand. Standard international delivery to Canada takes 5 to 8 business days; to the UK, EU, Australia and New Zealand it takes 7 to 14 business days. International shipping is a flat $19.95, free on orders over $200. Duties and import taxes are calculated at checkout and prepaid, so there are no surprise fees at the door.

## Carriers and tracking

Domestic orders ship with UPS or USPS depending on the destination; international orders ship with DHL Express. A tracking number is emailed as soon as the label is created, and tracking usually shows movement within 24 hours. You can also look up any order by its order number (format NW-12345) with our support assistant.

## Delayed or lost packages

If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, or the package is marked delivered but you cannot find it, contact support. We open an investigation with the carrier and, if the parcel is confirmed lost, we send a free replacement or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer.

## PO boxes and military addresses

USPS delivers to PO boxes and APO/FPO/DPO military addresses. Expedited and overnight options are not available for these addresses.
