# Lifetime Warranty

Every product we design is covered by the Northwind Promise: if it fails because of a defect in materials or workmanship, we repair it, replace it, or refund it for as long as you own it.

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers manufacturing defects such as failed zippers, delaminating seams, broken buckles, faulty stitching and waterproof membranes that leak under normal use. It applies to Northwind-branded products; third-party brands are covered by their manufacturer warranty.

## What is not covered

Normal wear and tear, damage from accidents or misuse, damage from improper washing, and products modified by the owner are not covered. Fading from sun exposure and pilling of fleece are considered normal wear.

## How to file a warranty claim

Contact support with your order number (or proof of purchase), your email address, a short description of the issue and, if possible, photos of the defect. Our team reviews claims within 2 business days. Approved claims receive a prepaid shipping label. Most repairs are completed within 3 weeks; if a repair is not possible we send a replacement of equal value or issue store credit.

## Repairs for non-warranty damage

Our Portland repair studio fixes accidental damage at cost. Typical prices: zipper slider replacement $15, small tear patch $20, full zipper replacement $45. Send a request to support to get a quote.
